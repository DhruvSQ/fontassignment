Exploring Fonts in CSS

How typography improves web design

Fonts play an important role in the way people experience a webpage. A readable font can make information easier to understand, while a poor font choice can make content difficult to follow.

CSS gives designers several ways to style text. Developers can use web safe fonts, connect to online font libraries such as Google Fonts, and even load downloaded fonts with the @font-face rule.

Good typography helps organize information and gives a webpage personality. When font families, sizes, and spacing are chosen carefully, a site looks more polished and professional.

"Typography is not just about making words visible. It is about making ideas clear and memorable."
  • Readable fonts improve user experience
  • Font choices affect mood and style
  • Line spacing helps paragraphs feel clear
  • Consistent typography creates unity
